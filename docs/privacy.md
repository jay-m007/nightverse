# Nightverse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Nightverse (the Windows app and the browser extension) shows lyrics for the song you are already playing.

## What leaves your device

To find lyrics, Nightverse sends the **current song's title, artist, album and length** to [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free public lyrics database. The browser extension also downloads the song's cover image from YouTube's image servers so it can pick colours from it.

That is the only data sent anywhere. Nightverse has:

- no accounts or sign-in,
- no analytics, tracking or advertising,
- no servers of its own.

## What stays on your device

- Your settings (lyric style, clock, glow) and a cache of lyrics already downloaded, stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Nightverse` (Windows app) or the browser's extension storage (extension).
- Nightverse reads "now playing" information from Windows or from the YT Music tab only to display it. It never records or uploads your listening history.

Uninstalling the app or extension deletes this data.
